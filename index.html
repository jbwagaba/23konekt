OUR JOURNEY

Company Timeline

"From Humble Beginnings to becoming a Leading IT & Security Systems in Uganda — This is our story of Growth and Achievement."

Founded in 2022, 23Konekt has grown into a leading IT & security systems integrator, protecting homes, businesses, institutions, and farms with intelligent technology.

Company Founded and Established

23Konekt IT & Security Systems was established with a vision to provide basic and quality IT Solutions for Homes, Business, and Offices.
